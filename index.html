SATOS Calendar Sync

SATOS Calendar Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner, Sat Nichapon. It connects to the owner's own Google Calendar to read upcoming events for a daily summary and to create calendar events that the owner asks for.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

  • What it does: lists and creates events in the owner's Google Calendar.
  • Google permission requested: Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar), nothing else.
